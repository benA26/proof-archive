# Proof gap — 2026-07-05 and 2026-07-06

_Operator record of an external-anchoring gap. History only; alters no pick, report, card, model, or forward_log._

## What happened
The Top-3 Picker reports for **2026-07-05** and **2026-07-06** were generated and published
locally **before racing** by local filesystem timestamp:

- **2026-07-05** — published **08:06:58 GMT Summer Time (BST)**, pre-race.
- **2026-07-06** — published **10:12:03 GMT Summer Time (BST)**, pre-race.

However, **neither was externally anchored (committed/pushed to the `top3-proof` GitHub remote)
before racing.** Cause: a **travel period with no reliable network path to GitHub** (the same
hotel-wifi / phone-hotspot connectivity problems recorded in `proof_gap_2026-07-04.md`, which
blocked both the Betwise SmartForm feed and GitHub). The archives sat locally, untracked, until
**2026-07-07**, when they were pushed as **late anchors** alongside that day's normal run.

## Consequence for the proof record
- A local timestamp / file mtime is self-attested and forgeable (`git commit --date`); it is **not**
  external evidence of pre-race publication.
- The external anchors for both days were created and pushed **late — on 2026-07-07, after racing
  settled** (`anchors/top3_2026-07-05.sha256.txt`, `anchors/top3_2026-07-06.sha256.txt`, each
  carrying an explicit LATE ANCHOR disclaimer). A post-race push proves the files **existed as of
  the push date only**, NOT that they existed before the races.
- **Therefore 2026-07-05 and 2026-07-06 do NOT count as clean pre-race proof and are EXCLUDED from
  the clean pre-race proof record.** Any results those days stand as outcomes but carry **no
  externally-verifiable pre-race provenance.**
